Me

I’m Phal Adamou, a builder and computer science student interested in artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, and the infrastructure that will shape the next generation of software.

I build, experiment, study, and write about ideas that I find worth pursuing.

My work sits at the intersection of AI, software systems, agents, and research. I’m particularly interested in what happens when software moves beyond responding to instructions and starts being able to reason, use tools, operate in environments, recover from failure, and accomplish meaningful work.

I care about understanding things deeply, building from first principles, and turning ideas into working systems.

I’m still early in the journey. That is precisely why I’m building aggressively, learning continuously, and thinking long-term.
